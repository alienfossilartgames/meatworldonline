PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 2026

Alien Fossil Art & Games ("we," "our," or "us") operates meatworldonline.com. We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping things simple: we do not actively collect, store, or sell your personal data. You can explore the rot and listen to the transmissions anonymously.

1. DATA WE DO NOT COLLECT

We do not use invasive tracking pixels, we do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your personal information to access our core lore, maps, or audio dramas.

2. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES & COOKIES

While we do not collect your data, our website relies on third-party infrastructure to function. These services may collect standard web traffic data (such as IP addresses and browser types) or use cookies:

  • GitHub Pages: Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitor IP addresses for security and performance routing.
  • Embedded Media: We use third-party media players (such as Spotify and YouTube) to stream our audio dramas. When you interact with these embedded players, those platforms may set their own tracking cookies and collect usage data according to their respective privacy policies.

3. EXTERNAL LINKS

Our website contains links to external platforms, including Patreon for memberships and external storefronts (e.g., Fourthwall) for merchandise. Once you click these links and leave meatworldonline.com, any data you provide is governed by the privacy policies of those specific platforms.
