THE ARCHITECTS OF THE ROT

ALIEN FOSSIL STUDIOS

Alien Fossil Studios is an independent creative workshop helmed by Paul and Bobby, dedicated to forging dark, uncompromising transmedia universes. We are masters of science fiction, fantasy, and horror, specializing in immersive audio dramas that build terrifying, decaying ecosystems from the ground up. We don't just tell stories; we pull you straight into the dark and leave you to survive the rot.

PROJECT: MEATWORLD

Meatworld is our flagship nightmare. What started as a grim concept has evolved into an expanding multimedia property. While the world actively grows through fully voice-acted audio transmissions and deep, interactive lore, our ultimate vision is the realization of the Meatworld video game. We are currently deep in the development trenches, forging the assets, mechanics, and stories of the wasteland.
