THE ARCHITECTS OF THE ROT

ALIEN FOSSIL STUDIOS

Alien Fossil Studios is an independent creative workshop helmed by Paul Gioacchini and Bobby Hermesch, dedicated to forging dark, uncompromising multimedia universes. We specialize in science fiction, fantasy, and horror, forging immersive audio dramas that build terrifying, decaying ecosystems from the ground up. We don't just tell stories; we pull you straight into the dark and leave you to survive the rot.

PROJECT: MEATWORLD

Meatworld is our flagship nightmare. What started as a grotesquely charming concept has evolved into an ever-expanding entity that seems to have a mind of its own. This strange and terrifying world actively grows through fully voice-acted audio transmissions and deep, interactive lore, in ways that surprise even us! We invite you to join us on this derranged journey, which we hope you enjoy listening to as much as we have enjoyed creating it.
