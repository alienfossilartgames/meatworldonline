THE ARCHITECTS OF THE ROT

ALIEN FOSSIL ART & GAMES

We are an independent creative studio helmed by Paul and Bobby, dedicated to building visceral, unapologetic transmedia universes. Our work bridges the gap between atmospheric narrative audio dramas and hardcore multiplayer survival experiences. We don't just design games; we construct decaying ecosystems from the ground up.

PROJECT: MEATWORLD

Meatworld is our flagship nightmare. What started as a grim concept has evolved into an expanding multimedia property. While the world actively grows through fully voice-acted audio transmissions and deep, interactive lore, our ultimate vision is the realization of the Meatworld video game. We are currently deep in the development trenches, forging the assets, mechanics, and stories of the wasteland.
