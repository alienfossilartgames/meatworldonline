LORE OF THE WASTES

THE RAPTURE OF ROT

When the MEAT first consumed the earth 200 years ago, survivors viewed it through the lens of Revelation—a literal Hell on Earth where the unworthy were left behind[cite: 5]. The oceans choked with fat and blood runoff, and the biosphere was forced to transition from photosynthesis to decay[cite: 8].

THE DEATH OF THE OLD GODS

In the decades following the ground zero event, the concept of a loving, omnipotent creator was abandoned[cite: 5]. The few ancient texts that survived were grossly misunderstood. Theological concepts like "the Word became Flesh" were taken literally, viewed as the prophetic curse that doomed humanity[cite: 5].

THE BONEDUSTERS

Religious zealots living in the colossal ribcages of The Maw. They have embraced the hostile environment as divine, grinding bone into paint and worshipping the central MEAT brain as divinity incarnate[cite: 5].

THE SEEP FOLK

The heartbeat of Meatworld. They live in symbiotic harmony with the living bogs, practicing quiet communion and listening to the pulse of the planet rather than fighting it[cite: 5].

THE CRUSTLANDERS

The innovators and pioneers of the sun-baked crust who skin the earth for Terrahide leather. They worship "The Spark" of human intellect and view the meat-scape merely as unrefined fuel[cite: 5].

THE SCABKINS

Nomadic warmongers in spiked war-rigs. They view the apocalypse as a great test, venerating scars as holy scripture and worshiping the "Great Adversary" through combat[cite: 5].

CARRION HOUNDS

Feral descendants of wild dogs and wolves. They have lost most of their hair, suffering from permanent mange, but boast incredibly high bite-forces to crush through bone to access marrow[cite: 8].

GORE-GATORS

Descendants of crocodilians that lost their scales in favor of thick, leathery, chemical-resistant hides. They ambush prey from beneath the surface of the seep bogs, entirely blind but hunting via vibrations in the sludge[cite: 8].

JERKY-RATS

Hairless rodents with iron-dense incisors capable of chewing through sun-baked muscle. They travel in massive swarms and extract hydration directly from the blood of whatever they kill[cite: 8].

SCAB-SCORPIONS

Massive, heavily armored arachnids that burrow through dried flesh in the Crustlands. Their stingers inject a digestive enzyme that liquifies the tough crust—or their prey—so they can drink it[cite: 8].

GOLIATH TAPEWORMS

Massive, blind, segmented horrors that tunnel through the organ walls of the Deepgut underworld, having thrived and mutated in the nutrient-dense environment[cite: 8].
